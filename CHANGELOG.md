## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-13

### Added

* Initial public release of **SWADE Weapon Properties**.
* Added support for custom weapon properties through Foundry Active Effects.

#### Brutal

* Added the **Brutal** damage property.
* Brutal damage dice reroll results of `1` until a result other than `1` is rolled.
* Brutal rerolls interact correctly with normal SWADE Acing:

  * A maximum result generated by a Brutal reroll can Ace.
  * A `1` generated by an Ace is rerolled by Brutal.
  * Chains of Brutal rerolls and Aces continue until resolved.
* Brutal applies to all dice contributing to a damage roll, including:

  * Strength damage.
  * Weapon/base damage.
  * Raise/Bonus Damage.
  * Conviction damage.
  * Custom bonus damage dice.
  * Other additional damage dice.
* Added support for Brutal damage when rerolling damage with a **Benny** or **Free Reroll**.
* Brutal dice are identified in damage roll displays with the `rr1` modifier.
* Added safeguards against runaway recursive dice rolls.
* Brutal can be applied using:

  * `flags.swade-weapon-properties.brutal`

#### Light

* Added the **Light** weapon property.
* Light weapons ignore SWADE's −2 Off-Hand Penalty when used in the off hand.
* Light applies only to the weapon carrying the property.
* Light does not grant or emulate the **Ambidextrous** Edge.
* Light does not alter SWADE's handling of Parry bonuses from multiple weapons.
* Light functions normally on characters that actually possess Ambidextrous.
* Light can be applied using:

  * `flags.swade-weapon-properties.light`
* Light and Brutal may be used simultaneously on the same weapon.

### Compatibility

* Foundry Virtual Tabletop v14.
* Savage Worlds Adventure Edition v6.0.4.
