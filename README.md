# SWADE Brutal Damage

A Foundry VTT module for **Savage Worlds Adventure Edition (SWADE)** that adds a **Brutal** property to damage rolls.

***DISCLAIMER:*** *This module was made using AI. I am not a coder, but it seems to be working correctly. If anyone who actually knows what they are doing sees some obvious problem, please feel free to let me know what it is and how to fix it and I will do so!*

## What Does Brutal Do?

Normally in SWADE, damage dice **Ace** when they roll their maximum value. The maximum result is kept and the die is rolled again. If the new result is also the maximum, it Aces again, continuing until a non-maximum result is rolled.

**Brutal** adds an additional rule:

> When a Brutal damage die rolls a **1**, the 1 is discarded and the die is rolled again. If another 1 is rolled, it is discarded and rolled again, continuing until the die rolls something other than 1.

Brutal does **not** replace normal SWADE Acing. Both rules work together.

For example, on a Brutal d6:

```text
1 → 1 → 4
```

The two 1s are discarded, for a final result of **4**.

```text
6 → 1 → 4
```

The 6 Aces, the 1 is discarded, and the 4 is retained, for a final result of **10**.

```text
1 → 6 → 6 → 1 → 3
```

The first 1 is discarded, both 6s Ace, the second 1 is discarded, and the 3 ends the chain, for a final result of **15**.

## All Damage Dice Are Brutal

When Brutal is active for a damage roll, it applies to **all dice that contribute to that damage roll**, not just the weapon's base damage die.

This includes:

* Weapon damage
* Strength damage
* Raise/Bonus Damage
* Conviction damage
* Additional damage dice added through modifiers
* Other bonus damage sources

For example, if a Brutal attack ultimately rolls:

```text
Strength:     1d8
Weapon:       1d6
Raise:        1d6
Conviction:   1d6
Bonus:        1d20
```

all five dice receive Brutal.

Brutal also remains active when the damage roll is **rerolled with a Benny or Free Reroll**.

## Using Brutal

Brutal is enabled through a Foundry **Active Effect**.

Just make an active effect with the flag listed below. If you use the first version, it will apply to ANY damage roll the actor makes. If you use the second, you can have it affect a specific weapon, just like how Trademark Weapon works.

| Attribute Key                              | Change Mode | Value  |
|--------------------------------------------|-------------|--------|
| `flags.swade-brutal-damage.brutal`         | Override    | `true` |
| `@Weapon{Weapon Name}[flags.swade-brutal-damage.brutal]` | Override  | `true` |

While that effect is active, applicable damage rolls will use the Brutal rules.

This allows Brutal to be used for things such as:

* Naturally Brutal creatures
* Brutal melee weapons
* Special ammunition
* Magical weapons
* Temporary powers or abilities
* Situational effects

Removing or disabling the Active Effect returns damage rolls to normal SWADE behavior.

## Dice Formula

Brutal dice are displayed in the Foundry chat card with the `rr1` modifier.

For example:

```text
1d6xrr1
```

Here:

* `1d6` is the damage die.
* `x` represents normal SWADE Acing.
* `rr1` represents the Brutal reroll behavior.

The module handles the interaction between these rules internally so that a Brutal reroll can Ace and a die generated by an Ace can itself trigger Brutal.

Currently developed and tested with:

## Installation

### Manifest Installation

In Foundry VTT:

1. Open **Add-on Modules**.
2. Select **Install Module**.
3. Paste the module's manifest URL into the **Manifest URL** field.
4. Select **Install**.

Manifest URL:

```text
https://github.com/bigbadbuffalo/swade-brutal-damage/releases/latest/download/module.json
```

After installation, enable **SWADE Brutal Damage** from **Manage Modules** inside your SWADE world.

### Manual Installation

Download the module release and place the `swade-brutal-damage` folder inside:

```text
FoundryVTT/Data/modules/
```

Restart Foundry VTT and enable the module from **Manage Modules**.
